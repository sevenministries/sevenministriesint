After-School Club

Safe, supportive learning beyond the classroom

Program Overview

Our After-School Club provides a structured, nurturing environment where children receive academic support, creative enrichment, and Christian mentorship after regular school hours.

What Your Child Gets

  • Homework support and academic guidance
  • Reading, numeracy, and creative activities
  • Christian values and character development
  • Safe, supervised learning environment

Who It’s For

Children aged 5–16 who need structured after-school care, academic reinforcement, and positive mentorship.
